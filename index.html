Our mission

To give the voters of East Fort Worth a voice in the issues that affect their daily lives.

About us

Neighbors working for accountable representation.

We are a nonpartisan group of individuals with a common goal: keeping the attention of our elected officials on the needs of the people they represent.

We strive to thoughtfully vet political candidates, weigh the needs of the community, and endorse candidates we believe will represent East Fort Worth through selfless service.

Good representation begins with listening. We want residents to share what is working, what is not, and what deserves more attention—because the people who live here understand this community best.
